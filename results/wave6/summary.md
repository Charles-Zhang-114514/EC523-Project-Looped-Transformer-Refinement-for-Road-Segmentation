## Scope and limitations

- TVS epoch is fixed at 2. TVS seed and cross-group selection explicitly use Val-rep LB under the registered rules; the selected score is optimistic.
- Descriptive TVS epoch peaks are never substituted for the predeclared checkpoint.
- CD Val-sel and TVS Val-rep epoch curves use different held-out pools and cannot isolate the cause of a peak shift.
- Manifest fingerprints are from final evaluation reports; training configurations record the common cache path, not independent startup fingerprints.
- CUDA peaks are saved allocated-memory peaks, not an external job-wide GPU monitor.
- The qacct call reads a local exact-job accounting snapshot; sandbox UID resolution failed, but the host-side read-only qacct call succeeded.
- Canceled duplicate jobs 8833713-8833721 are excluded.

Observed no new experiment, official metric implementation, inference or validation-driven epoch change was performed. Rounded preregistered baselines and their full-precision archives yield the same prediction verdicts; both numerical readings are retained in metrics.json.
